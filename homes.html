Visiting any care home? Ask these.

Whichever home you choose, these questions help you see how it really works.

  1. How many residents live here, and how many staff are on each shift?
  2. Are qualified nurses available round the clock?
  3. Who plans the meals, and can they handle my parent’s health needs?
  4. How are medicines given and recorded?
  5. How does the home reduce the risk of falls?
  6. Can I see the kitchen, rooms and washrooms?

Moving in, step by step

A calm, unhurried process, so you and your parent feel sure before any decision is made.

  1. Talk to us

    Call, message on WhatsApp or send an enquiry. Tell us about your parent’s health, habits and what worries you.

  2. Visit the home

    Meet the team, see the rooms and kitchen, and get a feel for the small-family atmosphere.

  3. Plan the care

    We build a personalised plan covering meals, medication, mobility and daily routines.

  4. Settle in

    Your parent moves in with all-day support, and you stay in touch with the care team.

Why a small home makes a big difference

With 8–10 residents, caregivers learn how each person likes their tea, when they wake up and what worries them. Larger homes rarely have the room for that.
