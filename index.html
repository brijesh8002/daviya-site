Care that feels personal, not institutional.

Daviya Elderly Care Homes, a unit of Daviya Care Foundation, is a specialised assisted living home for senior citizens. We care for body, mind, spirit and emotions together, because good health is more than medicine.

Every resident receives personalised, tailor-made care based on their day-to-day needs, not the one-size-fits-all routine of a crowded old age home. That is why every Daviya home in Delhi NCR keeps to a deliberately small size.

“Small Family is a Happy Family.”

Our guiding philosophy

  • Small by design

    No Daviya facility in Delhi NCR houses more than 8–10 people, which keeps the setting intimate and the attention close.

  • Personalised care

    Every resident has care built around their own needs and requirements, from meals to mobility to medication.

  • Holistic wellbeing

    Mental, emotional and spiritual wellbeing are supported through yoga, meditation and a warm, supportive home life.

Moving in, step by step

A calm, unhurried process, so you and your parent feel sure before any decision is made.

  1. Talk to us

    Call, message on WhatsApp or send an enquiry. Tell us about your parent’s health, habits and what worries you.

  2. Visit the home

    Meet the team, see the rooms and kitchen, and get a feel for the small-family atmosphere.

  3. Plan the care

    We build a personalised plan covering meals, medication, mobility and daily routines.

  4. Settle in

    Your parent moves in with all-day support, and you stay in touch with the care team.
